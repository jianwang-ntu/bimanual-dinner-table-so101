The task

Open the top drawer, pick up the fork and the spoon and lay them either side of the place setting, put the plate on the mat, then set the mug down to the right of the plate.

Two SO-101 arms, MuJoCo, 10 randomized evaluation seeds, 5 scored sub-goals per seed.

The blind row is the negative control: it hands the controller a fixed nominal layout. If the controller ignored what it was handed, all three rows would be identical. perceived scores below privileged, and that is the direction stated here rather than the other way round.

What does not work

  • Task success is 0 of 10 seeds. No episode completes the whole instruction.
  • The fork and the spoon are placed on 0 of 10 seeds -- the perception network has no output for either object.
  • The mug reaches its mat on 1 of 10 seeds, and on the seed it does it arrives lying on its side.
  • The learned policy loses to the script. A LeRobot ACT policy behaviour-cloned on the scripted controller's own rollouts scores 3 of 50 sub-goals against the scripted controller at 15, on the same seeds and the same scorer, with more simulator time per episode. Everything shown on this page is the scripted controller.
  • No measurement was taken on Intel Core Ultra silicon. The OpenVINO numbers in the repository were produced on an AMD EPYC host and the bench script stamps them NOT_THE_REQUIRED_MEASUREMENT.
  • Natural-language interpretation, multi-step task context and re-planning are absent. The instruction is fixed and is not parsed.
